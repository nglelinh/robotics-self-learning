## Core ideas

Fixed 50–100 Hz loops; measure jitter; avoid long delay().



## Control notes

Start open-loop teleop; add encoder PID only after counts are trustworthy. Log loop period. MQTT/Wi-Fi adds latency—keep a local serial failsafe.
