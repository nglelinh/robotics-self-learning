## Core ideas

Stripped TT gears, binding, loose hubs, wire fatigue—inspect after crashes.



## Mechanism checklists

After assembly: spin shafts by hand (binding?), check set screws, verify gear mesh backlash isn’t catastrophic, add threadlocker only where intentional, route cables with service loops.


## Field failure gallery (what to look for)

Cracked TT gear teeth, ovalized mounting holes, melted motor driver smell, frayed Dupont at strain points, missing wheel set screws after a drop.
