## Core ideas

Four-bar DOF; timing belt pitch; rack-and-pinion linearization.



## Mechanism checklists

After assembly: spin shafts by hand (binding?), check set screws, verify gear mesh backlash isn’t catastrophic, add threadlocker only where intentional, route cables with service loops.


## When to use what

- **Gears:** compact ratio, backlash management needed
- **Belts:** quieter, need tension and pitch match
- **Four-bar:** path shaping / folding mechanisms
- **Rack-and-pinion:** steering, linear axes
